# 2026-10-27 — inphirev: the golden all-pass memory

New plugin of the family of STUNEDREV, for the *Dialogo Angelico* (canto alla durata).
Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-inphirev-plugin-design.md`; branch `feat/inphirev`.

## Decisions (Giuseppe and the agent, 2026-10-27)

- A-format throughout: independent chains per capsule, no B-format detour.
- g = 1/φ fixed, Moorer's form; no adaptivity.
- One golden spiral through the four faces, d(i, k) = d₀·φ^(i + k/4), each delay the prime strictly above the product.
- Distance on a per-id grid of φ^¼ steps (van der Corput phases), so that two STONEs never share a delay.
- New `seam.silvi.lib` (prefix `sgs`); the speed of sound c(T) is additive, `isos` and `esos` untouched (issue #15).
- A change of setting applies after 250 ms and runs a RESET.

## Work

Nine tasks, each tested and verified by mutation (`doc/study/inphirev/mutations.md`): speed of sound, Faust spec and reference, delay table and engine, ParamBox and state, decay estimate, processor and window, documentation.

## Measured and found

- The printed decay runs about 10 % long against the engine (t30 estimated/measured at (1, 10, 12), 48 kHz: 18.1/16.5, 20.4/18.4, 23.0/20.7, 26.0/23.4 s): the estimate treats the echoes as incoherent.
- Ids 1 and 2 never share a delay at 44.1/48/96/192 kHz; other pairs (phases ⅛ or ¼ apart) can, up to all 225 grid pairs at 48 kHz for ids 2/7, 3/5, 4/8.
  Proposal, not implemented: each id takes its next prime from its own residue class mod 16.
- CPU at 18 sections: 3.6 % of a core at 96 kHz, 1.6 % at 48 kHz, 3.3 % after 10 min of silence (i7-8850H, engine alone).
- Footer at the defaults: arena 289 MiB at 48 kHz, 577 MiB at 96 kHz; t30 A/B/C/D 201/227/256/289 s, t60 355/401/452/510 s.

## Carried fix

`setParamNormalized`: when the STONE id changes, the distance text changes too; the plugin now calls `restartComponent(kParamValuesChanged)` so the host re-reads it.
Compile-checked only (no Xcode); not exercised at runtime.
