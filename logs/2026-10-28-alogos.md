# 2026-10-27/28 — alogos: the irrational all-pass memory

ALOGOS replaces INPHIREV in the family "Works — canto alla durata": the golden spiral opened to four irrational scales and three densities.
Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-alogos-plugin-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-alogos.md`; branch `feat/alogos` in `seam-ltm` and `faust-libraries`.

## Decisions (Giuseppe and the agent, 2026-10-27)

- Geometry for every scale: φ, √2, e and π enter as the ratio r of a geometric spiral, not as STUNEDREV's arithmetic ladder.
- Two parameters shape the spiral: the scale r and the density D ∈ {1, 2, 4}, ρ = r^(1/D); INPHIREV is r = φ, D = 1.
- g is a menu of its own: 1/φ, 1/√2, 1/e, 1/π, √(√2−1); fixed per setting.
- The grid follows the density and the ids stay apart; the grids do not nest across densities (agent's call, builder check).
- Distance stored in metres (1–6 m), snapped to the grid of (id, r, D); sections 1–128, limited to N_max by a 90 s cap on the longest delay.
- Memory sized per setting, allocated by a worker thread, never in the audio thread; no memory budget (one ALOGOS per machine).
- Contacts between STONEs after rounding to primes are accepted; the mod-16 residue rule proposed for INPHIREV is withdrawn.
- One plugin: INPHIREV leaves the tree, v0.6.0 keeps it; the suite stays at twenty-one; release v0.7.0.

## Measured and found

- CPU (i7-8850H, engine alone, `doc/study/alogos/cpu.cpp`): default 1.3 % of a core at 48 kHz, 2.8 % at 96 kHz, 2.7 % after ten minutes of silence; heaviest (√2, D 4, 1 m, N 119, 476 sections per sample) 21.8 % at 96 kHz, arena 2.9 GiB.
- Arena of the heaviest setting per scale and density, at 96 kHz: from 256 MiB (π, D 1) to 3075 MiB (√2, D 4); 6.0 GiB at 192 kHz (`doc/study/alogos/arena.cpp`).
- Contacts among ids 1–4 at 96 kHz: longest shared delay φ 3.2/14.2/24.3 ms at D 1/2/4, √2 D 4 56 ms, e and π at most 14.2 ms.
- Within one instance the dense spirals repeat some short delays: √2 D 4 at 96 kHz, 344 repeats summed over the 410 settings of ids 0–4, at most 5 in one setting, all at or below 14.2 ms.
- The decay estimate was right all along: INPHIREV's "10 % long" came from measuring the backward-integrated tail energy, a second moment; measured as a level, the error is at most 1.4 %.
- The effective number of echoes peaks at g = √(√2−1) (3.188); 1/φ reaches 99.05 % of it, 1/√2 94 %, 1/e 53 %, 1/π 47 %.
